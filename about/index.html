about_the_project

This site is an outcome of my master's research on neologisms and word formation in a 4chan corpus. It connects research networks with original posts.

what_is_a_constructicon

In Construction Grammar, a constructicon is a network of constructions: pairings of form and meaning, connecting specific expressions with general patterns.

Here, the networks map morphological families, schemas and semantic groups, linking neologisms with examples of use.

exploring_the_network

Read the map. Colors mark families; node sizes use a natural-log frequency scale. Links show word-formation relationships.

Find examples. Search a fragment such as water, then select a word for available posts and family connections.

Drag to pan · scroll to zoom · FIT to reset.

a_note_on_the_data

This is a research corpus, not a complete 4chan archive. Posts retain their year and thread. Some graph entries have no matching post.

Content note. Offensive, hateful or explicit language is preserved as research material, not endorsed.
