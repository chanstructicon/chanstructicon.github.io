This site is an outcome of my master's research on neologisms and word formation in a 4chan corpus. It connects research networks with original posts.

what_is_a_constructicon

In Construction Grammar, a constructicon is a network of constructions: pairings of linguistic form and meaning. It connects specific expressions with more general patterns, rather than separating vocabulary from grammar.

this_constructicon

The main network maps morphological families. Specific networks unpack schemas, processes and semantic groups.

Together, they link word-formation patterns, their neologisms and examples of use.

reading_the_network

On the main map, colors distinguish families and node sizes use a natural-log frequency scale. Shared words can have blended colors.

Edges represent word-formation relationships, not relationships between users. The map is not a timeline.

from_word_to_context

Search a fragment such as water, then select a word to read its posts.

On specific networks, comments appear beside the graph and the selected node's parent path is highlighted. Zoom, pan or use FIT to reset the view.

a_note_on_the_data

This is a research corpus, not a complete 4chan archive. Posts retain their year and thread. Some graph entries have no matching post.

Content note. Offensive, hateful or explicit language is preserved as research material, not endorsed.
